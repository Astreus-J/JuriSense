# JuriSense — Plane Project Description

This is the canonical project description pushed to Plane by `scripts/plane/bootstrap.py`. Edit
here, not in the Plane UI, so the two stay in sync (see `docs/07-engineering/project-management.md`
for the workflow).

---

**JuriSense** is an AI-powered vendor, contract, and compliance operations platform that transforms
supplier data, contracts, company policies, external evidence, and human approvals into explainable
and auditable compliance decisions.

The initial commercial product is **JuriSense Gate — Vendor & Contract Compliance**.

Every review resolves to one of:

**APPROVE · REVIEW · BLOCK**

— always backed by **Evidence, Policy, Source, Risk, Required Action, Approver, Audit Trail, and
Verification** (see `docs/00-project/overview.md`).

Repository documentation (`docs/`) is the source of truth for architecture, product concepts,
technical decisions, business rules, and ADRs. **This Plane project is the source of truth for work
planning, execution, priorities, progress, milestones, dependencies, and releases.**

Roadmap: 12 phases, Foundation through International (`docs/09-planning/roadmap.md`). The
**Legal Hack 2026** submission (BLI Legal Tech Hackathon 2 / Legal Hack 2026, judging
2026-11-05–2026-12-05) is a milestone inside this roadmap — the **Hackathon Cut** falls at the end
of Phase 8 — not the definition of the project. Filter by the `scope:hackathon` label or the
**Hackathon** module to see hackathon-critical work; everything else is the ongoing commercial
roadmap.

**Engineering language policy**: everything in this project — code, comments, tests, commits,
branches, PRs, and every Plane issue/module/label/state — is written in English. See
`docs/07-engineering/coding-standards.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
